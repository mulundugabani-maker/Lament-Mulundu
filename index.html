“I write not because I understand life, but because I am constantly trying to understand what it means to live.”

My name is Lament Mulundu, a Zambian writer, storyteller, and Bachelor of Science in Marketing student at the Copperbelt University.

I am fascinated by human experiences, philosophy, literature, and the complexities of existence. I write poetry, reflections, personal essays, and fiction about love, loss, faith, identity, and the silent battles that shape who we become.

Personal Reflection

The Uncertainty of Becoming

Perhaps one of the most difficult aspects of being human is knowing that we are constantly becoming something, yet never knowing precisely what that something will be. We make plans, develop expectations, and imagine futures, only to discover that life has its own interpretation of our intentions.

    Privacy

    When you create an account, your name and email are stored so you can log in. Your saved pieces, reading progress and comments are stored with it, so they follow you across phones.

    If you add a photo, a short bio or a location in your profile, those are stored too. They are optional and you can remove them at any time.

    The site owner can see which pieces are opened and finished, when people sign in, and which contact links are tapped. This is used only to understand what is read, and is never sold or shared.

    You will only get emails about new pieces if you switch that on in Settings. You can delete your account and data at any time under Profile › Settings.
