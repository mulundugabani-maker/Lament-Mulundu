01 · About Me

Words, thoughts and stories from an unfinished journey.

Exploring human experiences, philosophy, literature, and the silent battles that shape who we become.

“I write not because I understand life, but because I am constantly trying to understand what it means to live.”

My name is Lament Mulundu, a Zambian writer, storyteller, and Bachelor of Science in Marketing student at the Copperbelt University.

I am fascinated by human experiences, philosophy, literature, and the complexities of existence. My writing explores the things we often struggle to articulate: love, loss, faith, identity, uncertainty, relationships, and the silent battles that shape who we become.

I write poetry, philosophical reflections, personal essays, and fiction. My approach to writing is deeply introspective, often questioning the meaning of life, the nature of human relationships, and our search for purpose in an unpredictable world.

An introspective exploration of existence, consciousness, suffering, and the peculiar burden of being human. The work examines the questions we inherit simply by existing and the difficult search for meaning in a world that rarely provides straightforward answers.

A coming-of-age Christian existential story following Lubuto, a young student navigating friendship, love, faith, mortality, and the difficult questions surrounding human existence. It explores the intersection of youthful innocence, spiritual awakening, and the search for truth.

Personal Essay · Literary Reflection

Love, Control and the Things We Call Affection

An exploration of the complicated relationship between love and control. It questions whether affection is always an expression of genuine love or whether, sometimes, our desire to keep someone close becomes an attempt to possess them.
